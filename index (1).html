Design thinking project · INT 335

A small direction can make a big difference.

The project focuses on reducing uncertainty for people who know the block number but do not know how to reach it.

👥 Who is this for?

New students, parents, campus visitors and delivery personnel who are unfamiliar with LPU campus routes.

  • First-time freshers looking for a classroom
  • Parents and guests visiting the university
  • Delivery personnel locating a destination

💡 The proposed solution

Combine clear physical guidance with a simple QR-based route guide that explains the next direction using recognisable landmarks.

  • Start from a clearly identified point
  • Show one direction at a time
  • Confirm arrival and collect feedback
SCAN & START

Directions, one scan away.

In the final campus prototype, place a QR code at an approved information point. Visitors can scan it to open the Block 34 guide directly on their phone. This demo QR represents the website address once hosted online.
